06 / 07

WALLY

This is not an AI problem.

It is a design problem.

  • Organisations route insight through assumption, not proximity — and call it research.
  • Expertise without lived experience builds things that are beautiful and wrong.
  • You design for the world you expect. Your users inhabit a different one.

Spotted Ape — Design Strategy

07 / 07

Stop narrating.

Start perceiving.

  1. Reason natively in context
    Don’t translate lived experience into abstraction before acting on it.
  1. Proximity is a design method
    Build teams structurally close to the problem before generating solutions.
  1. Design with, not for
    The gap between participation and observation is where products fail.
